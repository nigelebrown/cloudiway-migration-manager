New Migration Upload

Every uploaded file becomes its own workflow batch and the app automatically creates a matching Cloudiway Mail Batch number.

1. UploadValidate users and create batch.
2. RackspacePasswords generated automatically for manual bulk mode.
3. CloudiwayUsers, credentials and batch membership prepared automatically.
4. MigrateStart automatically or with one batch-level button.

Standard columns: source_email, target_email, first_name, last_name, computer_number.

Rackspace format is also accepted: Username becomes the source email; append TargetEmail/DestinationEmail and ComputerNumber. Passwords in the initial upload are ignored.
