New Migration Upload

Upload the full master list once using the standard identity fields. The app stages all valid users without changing AD or generating passwords. You then release controlled groups through AD identity validation, provisioning, Microsoft 365 readiness, Rackspace, and Cloudiway. Each released migration group receives its own Cloudiway Mail Batch number.

Download the standard JCF upload template

1. UploadStage the full master list.
2. SelectChoose the next quantity or exact users.
3. RackspaceGenerate/download passwords only for that group.
4. CloudiwayRe-upload confirmation, prepare, migrate and monitor.

Required columns: computer_number, source_email, target_email, first_name, last_name. Optional: middle_name.

Computer Number is a required identity control. Existing AD users are automatically accepted only when email/UPN, Computer Number, and first/last name identify the same enabled AD object. Partial or conflicting matches are held for individual manual review.
