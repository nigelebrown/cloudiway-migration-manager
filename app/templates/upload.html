New Migration Upload

Upload the full master list once. The app stages all valid users without generating passwords. You then choose the next quantity (for example 5, 20, or 50) or select exact users. Each released migration group receives its own Cloudiway Mail Batch number.

1. UploadStage the full master list.
2. SelectChoose the next quantity or exact users.
3. RackspaceGenerate/download passwords only for that group.
4. CloudiwayRe-upload confirmation, prepare, migrate and monitor.

Standard columns: source_email, target_email, first_name, last_name, computer_number.

Rackspace format is also accepted: Username becomes the source email; append TargetEmail/DestinationEmail and ComputerNumber. Passwords in the initial upload are ignored.
